From headline to diagram

Every news story is an exam question in disguise.

Example headline · for practice

“Central bank raises interest rates by 2 percentage points to tackle inflation”

AO1Knowledge
AO2Analysis
AO2Diagram
AO3Evaluation
01 · Name the policy

This is contractionary monetary policy: the central bank raises the cost of borrowing to reduce total spending in the economy.

Knowledge: say exactly what the policy is before anything else.
02 · Build the chain

Higher interest rates make loans and mortgages more expensive and saving more rewarding, so consumption and investment fall, which means aggregate demand falls.

Analysis: every step linked to the next with because… so… which means…
03 · Draw it

On an AD/AS diagram, AD shifts left to AD1. The price level falls from PL to PL1 and real GDP falls from Y to Y1.

Both axes labelled, both curves labelled, old and new equilibrium marked.
04 · Judge it

Inflation should ease, but growth slows and unemployment may rise. How far depends on the size of the rise, how much households have borrowed, and time lags of up to two years.

Evaluation: a clear judgement, and what it depends on.
