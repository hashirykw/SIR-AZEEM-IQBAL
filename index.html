How an essay earns its marks

A 26-mark essay is an argument you weigh.

Practice essay · 9699 Paper 2 style · 26 marks

“Evaluate the view that the nuclear family is the best type of family for meeting the needs of society.”

AO1Knowledge
AO2Application
AO3Evaluation
AO3Judgement
01 · Set out the view

Functionalists argue the nuclear family suits modern society. Murdock lists four universal functions, and Parsons says it provides primary socialisation and stabilises adult personalities.

AO1: name the perspective, the sociologists and their concepts accurately.
02 · Apply it

The New Right backs the nuclear ideal and links lone-parent families to welfare dependency. But apply it to a real society: in Pakistan many households are joint or extended, and they also socialise children and care for elders.

AO2: use the evidence on the actual question, with real examples.
03 · Weigh the other side

Feminists such as Oakley, and Delphy and Leonard, say the nuclear family exploits women's unpaid work. Marxists say it serves capitalism. Postmodernists such as Stacey say diversity is now normal.

AO3: compare perspectives and question the evidence, point by point.
04 · Reach a judgement

The nuclear family meets some needs, but “best” depends on whose needs and which society. Family diversity and the evidence on unequal roles weaken the claim that one type suits everyone.

AO3: answer the question directly. A judgement, not a summary.
