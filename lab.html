Draw it, calculate it, then explain it.

Five tools for the skills Economics papers test every series: shifting supply and demand, elasticity, the production possibility curve, macro calculations and key terms. Every result shows its working, ready to copy into an answer.

Every market event, on one diagram.

Choose what happens. The new curve draws in, the new equilibrium is marked, and you get the written explanation in the order examiners reward it.

O Level 2.2–2.10 · A Level 2.1–3.2

Work out the elasticity, then say what it means.

Enter the old and new values. You get the percentage changes, the coefficient, whether it is elastic or inelastic, and what happens to revenue.

O Level 2.6–2.7 · A Level 2.2–2.3

Read opportunity cost off the curve.

Slide along the production possibility curve. Moving from one point to another shows what must be given up. Then shift the curve to see growth, or move inside it to see unemployment.

The macro numbers, with the working.

Inflation from a price index, the unemployment rate, GDP per head, the growth rate and, for A Level, the multiplier. Change any figure and every answer updates.
